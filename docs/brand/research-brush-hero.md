# Research: "Brush Hero" brand availability

**Researched:** 2026-04-28 (background agent)

## TL;DR

**Verdict: CAUTIOUS / leaning BLOCKED. Drop from shortlist.**

Two independent material conflicts:

1. **"BRUSH HERO" is a live USPTO registration** (Reg. No. 4908058, Class 7) — owned by Habanero Pepper Projects, Inc., the Shark Tank water-powered cleaning brush. ~$6M/yr revenue, Costco partnership, brand still active in 2026. Different class from ours, but they have enforcement budget to oppose.

2. **"Brushing Hero" by LITALICO Inc.** is already a kids' toothbrushing app on iOS + Google Play + Amazon, with camera-based monster-defeating gameplay. **This is functionally the same product as Brush Quest in our exact category.** 100K+ downloads, 4.0 stars, active in 2025. A USPTO examiner doing a 2(d) likelihood-of-confusion analysis would almost certainly cite it.

**Probability of clean class-9/41 USPTO registration:** ~20-30%.
**Probability of avoiding cease-and-desist or rebrand pressure within 24 months:** ~50%.

## Detail by axis

### App stores
- Apple App Store: **Brushing Hero (LITALICO)** is the dominant near-match in our exact niche. 37 results for "brush hero", no exact title match but Brushing Hero is the first relevant.
- Google Play: **Brushing Hero** surfaces first. 100K+ downloads, 4.0 stars. Available globally.

### USPTO
- **BRUSH HERO** confirmed registered: Serial 86671948, Reg 4908058, Class 7 ("water-powered cleaning brush"), Owner Habanero Pepper Projects, Inc., filed 2015-06-23, Status 700 (Live).
- No identical mark in classes 9 or 41 in publicly indexed records.
- However, a US examiner would likely cite **Brushing Hero** (LITALICO's common-law mark in identical channels of trade) against any "Brush Hero" Class 9/41 application.

### Cleaning-brush company (Habanero Pepper Projects / Brush Hero)
- Founders: Kevin Williams & Glenn Archer
- Founded: 2015 (Kickstarter) — Shark Tank Season 9 (2017)
- Class 7 only (no Class 9/21/41 overlap with our app's filing target)
- Active in 2026 (brand site, Amazon storefront, retail)
- Threat model: opposition during USPTO publication = $5-25K legal cost, not winnable on merits but real friction

### Cultural / branding fit
- "Hero" theme aligns well with kids' Space Rangers framing
- Google searches for "Brush Hero" surface the cleaning brand first → we'd compete for our own brand name in SEO/ads
- "Brushing Hero" already takes our exact functional positioning

### Competitive landscape side-finding
LITALICO's "Brushing Hero" is a direct competitor in the kids' camera-based toothbrushing-game space. Worth tracking separately in the competitive landscape file (not just the brand file). Their existence:
- Validates the product concept
- Means the category is small enough that a Japanese publisher hasn't dominated US — opportunity remains
- Means we should ASO-differentiate from them (not just visually but in keyword choice)

## Recommended posture

**Drop "Brush Hero" from the candidate shortlist.** The combination of (a) the live $6M cleaning-brush trademark with exact wording, (b) the near-identical kids' toothbrushing app already in our category on all three stores, and (c) Google SERP dominance by the cleaning brand makes this the highest-friction option among likely alternatives. "Brush Quest" is materially safer than this.

If a "hero" frame is appealing, alternatives that clear most conflicts: "Tooth Hero", "Cavity Hero", "Brushy Hero" — each needs its own check.
