## Strategic options (ranked)

### Option A — Contact Mitchell Pothitakis directly (RECOMMENDED first move)

Send a courteous email/iMessage offering to acquire the app, the name, or both. Frame: "I've launched a similar app under the same name; willing to compensate you to consolidate."

**Pros:**
- Cleanest legal resolution — gets us the iOS bundle ID, his common-law trademark rights, any user data
- Likely cheap if he's truly abandoned ($500-2000 range)
- Signals good faith vs. bullying
- Closes the conflict permanently

**Cons:**
- Risk he's emotionally attached → asks for absurd money or refuses
- Risk this discloses our intent and triggers him to file his own trademark
- Communication slow — could take weeks

**Timeline:** Outreach today. Decision after his response (or 14-day non-response window).

### Option B — File our trademark in parallel, defensive (FALLBACK for A)

While waiting on Mitchell, file USPTO TEAS Plus application for "BRUSH QUEST" in classes 9 (downloadable apps for kids' hygiene games) + 41 (entertainment services — online games). ~$500 if DIY.

**Pros:**
- Establishes our claim if Mitchell never filed
- Strengthens negotiating position with Mitchell
- 6-12 months to grant — we get filing date priority immediately

**Cons:**
- If Mitchell has earlier filing in same class, ours could be challenged
- Doesn't resolve marketing collision — both apps coexist

**Timeline:** File within 14 days. Optional: 30-min IP attorney consult ($200-400) before filing.

### Option C — Rebrand (CONTINGENCY)

If Option A fails AND we discover Mitchell has filed/asserted rights, rebrand to a new name. Candidates need to be: short, parent-pronounceable, kids-pronounceable, available across all stores + domains + handles.

**Pros:**
- Eliminates legal risk entirely
- Chance to pick stronger, more distinctive brand

**Cons:**
- 2-3 weeks of asset rework: app icon, listing, screenshots, promo video end card, landing page, all marketing copy
- Lose "Brush Quest" SEO/discovery momentum (modest given pre-launch state)
- Re-submit Play Store listing for review (another 24-48h cycle)
- iOS submission needs to use new name from start

**Timeline:** If triggered, 2-3 weeks before re-launching public assets.

### Option D — Monitor + defer (NOT RECOMMENDED at this severity)

Continue with current name, file trademark in 6-12 months once traction. Risk competitor activates and asserts rights when we have something to lose.

**Why not recommended:** the cost of waiting is asymmetric — by the time we have traction worth defending, his case (or a third party's) is also stronger.
